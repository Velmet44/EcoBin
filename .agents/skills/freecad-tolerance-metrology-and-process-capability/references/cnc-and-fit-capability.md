# CNC and fit capability

Qualify the finished feature, not only the machine. Include stock condition, material, machine, setup, workholding, tool and wear state, coolant, thermal stabilization, datum transfer, probing, deburr, stress relief, heat treatment, coating, and final inspection.

ISO 230-1 and ISO 10791-7 machine/test-piece results may support a capability assessment, but they do not prove the actual part route. Cross-setup position, slender features, bore geometry, interrupted cuts, clamping distortion, residual stress, and post-coat size need part-representative evidence.

For a precision interface:

- Define the functional fit and worst-case operating state.
- Allocate size, form, orientation, location, surface, and thermal/coating contributions.
- Identify which mating item is controlled and replaceable.
- Define assembly force, lubrication, retention, inspection, and service consequences.
- Finish bearing bores, sealing datums, and other critical interfaces after distortion-producing operations when the route requires it.

ISO 286-1/-2 can define limits and fits for applicable cylindrical/parallel interfaces. It does not establish that a selected process can hold them. Thread tolerance classes likewise require a qualified forming/cutting/inspection route.
