# Router, time, cost, and production risk

## Operation record

Each operation should name:

- sequence and predecessors;
- input/output product state and quantity;
- machine/work center, fixture, tools, program/document revision;
- setup, machine, labor, inspection, rework, queue/cure/outsource time;
- characteristics created and inspected;
- yield, scrap, rework, evidence, and acceptance authority;
- special-process qualification and lot/serial traceability where applicable.

Keep design parameters, process-plan targets, and shop-authorized parameters distinct.

## Cost normalization

Normalize alternative routes to the same:

- accepted quantity and configuration;
- currency/date and commercial basis;
- recurring/non-recurring boundary;
- material, scrap/remnant credit, yield and rework assumptions;
- labor/machine burden;
- inspection, special process, packaging, logistics, and disposal.

Break-even for alternatives A and B, when variable costs differ:

`Q = (fixed_B - fixed_A) / (variable_A - variable_B)`

Report when the denominator is zero, quantity is negative, or capacity/lead-time invalidates the mathematical result.

## Risk and validation

Check:

- capability on critical characteristics;
- datum transfer and tolerance stack by operation;
- process stability, tool wear, distortion, and special-process variability;
- inspection access and measurement uncertainty;
- failure/rework paths and maximum allowed rework;
- supplier qualification, capacity, and second-source strategy;
- packaging damage, corrosion, ESD, FOD, humidity, and transport loads.

Use PFMEA/control-plan artifacts where the quality system requires them. A low expected cost does not excuse a safety, capability, or traceability gap.

## Manufacturing KPI context

[ISO 22400-1:2014](https://www.iso.org/standard/56847.html), confirmed current in 2025, provides an industry-neutral framework and terminology for manufacturing-operations KPIs. Apply only the KPIs and definitions invoked by the organization; this skill’s calculations are transparent planning metrics, not a claim of ISO conformity.
