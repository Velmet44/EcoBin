# AM design, post-process, and inspection

Treat property and geometry states explicitly. Heat treatment, HIP, support removal, machining, polishing, coating, and conditioning can change size, surface, residual stress, porosity response, and allowables. Inspect at the state that controls function and repeat after any operation that can alter the CTQ.

Use witness coupons and artifacts representative of build location, orientation, section thickness, thermal history, material reuse, and post-process. Internal channels, lattices, and inaccessible surfaces require validated cleaning and inspection methods.

ISO/ASTM 52902:2023 covers geometric capability artifacts. ISO/ASTM 52908:2023 addresses post-processing, inspection, and testing of metal PBF parts. ISO/ASTM 52927:2024 links AM quality characteristics to tests and supply agreements.
