# AM process and qualification

Select the process only after requirements, material state, quantity, envelope, feature scale, surface, internal geometry, allowable defects, post-processing, inspection, and economics are defined. MEX/FDM, vat photopolymerization, powder-bed polymer processes, binder jetting, directed-energy deposition, and metal powder-bed fusion have different hazards and qualification needs.

ISO/ASTM 52910:2018 provides general design guidance but not universal process/material values. ISO/ASTM 52920:2023 covers qualification and quality assurance principles for industrial AM sites. ISO/ASTM 52901:2017 addresses buyer/provider exchange of part definition, feedstock, final characteristics, inspection, and acceptance requirements.
