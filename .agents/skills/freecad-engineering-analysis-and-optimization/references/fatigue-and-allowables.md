# Fatigue and allowable discipline

Use this reference when comparing calculated response with strength, life, or stability criteria.

## Match demand to allowable

Record whether each value is:

- Typical property, minimum specification value, statistically derived design allowable, supplier rating, code allowable, or test-derived value.
- Yield, ultimate, proof, bearing, shear, creep, rupture, fatigue, fracture, adhesive, weld, or joint data.
- Valid for the product form, thickness, direction, temperature, surface, environment, heat treatment, joining process, and lot/population.

Apply factors and knockdowns once. Keep the raw allowable, each reduction, the factored allowable, and the governing design factor visible.

## Fatigue record

- Mission profile and spectrum source.
- Stress/strain extraction location and method.
- Elastic/plastic correction and notch treatment.
- Rainflow or other cycle-counting method.
- Mean-stress correction and multiaxial criterion.
- S-N, E-N, crack-growth, or code method and curve revision.
- Surface, size, temperature, reliability, corrosion, weld-class, and manufacturing modifiers.
- Damage rule and sequence/loading interaction assumptions.
- Inspection interval, detectable flaw, retirement rule, and scatter.

Mesh convergence of a nominal peak does not remove a geometric singularity. Use a defined structural/hot-spot stress, notch, fracture-mechanics, or code method compatible with the selected fatigue data.

## Stability

Treat a linear eigenvalue as an ideal-model screening result. Establish imperfection amplitude/shape, residual stress, load eccentricity, material nonlinearity, contact, and boundary stiffness for nonlinear confirmation. Correlate slender or safety-critical structures through physical testing when required.

## Controlling sources

Prefer the exact customer, regulatory, industry-code, material-handbook, or statistically controlled internal source named by the design authority. Confirm revision and licensing; do not reproduce proprietary allowable tables from memory.
