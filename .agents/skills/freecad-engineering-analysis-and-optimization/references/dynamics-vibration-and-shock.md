# Dynamics, vibration, and shock evidence

Match the verification to the physics and release-driving quantity:

- Modal: rigid-body modes, mass normalization, effective mass/participation by direction, extraction range, mode tracking and boundary/preload/contact sensitivity.
- Harmonic/FRF: excitation definition, damping provenance, frequency resolution/refinement, phase, resonance peak convergence and comparison to a simple dynamic model or test.
- Random vibration: PSD units and axis/cross-spectral assumptions, integration bandwidth, RMS calculation, statistical peak basis, fatigue-duration mapping and fixture/notching limits.
- Transient: integrator, time step/adaptation, numerical damping, event reconstruction, force/displacement histories, energy balance and step/tolerance sensitivity.
- Shock: pulse definition and sampling, fixture/input measurement, SRS method/damping, peak timing and bandwidth-appropriate stress/contact interpretation.
- Flexible-body robot loads: modal reduction range/participation, interface frames, load interpolation, time/event IDs and reconciliation to the multibody/controls model.

FreeCAD/CalculiX/Elmer exposure is version- and formulation-dependent. Confirm the actual solver deck, formulation and recovered result; GUI availability does not establish suitability.

Use controlled V&V procedures and independent physical correlation. ASME V&V 10-2019 (R2025) provides general computational solid-mechanics guidance:
